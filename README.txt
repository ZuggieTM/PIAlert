PI ALERT

PI Alert highlights eligible party and raid members when they activate configured
major cooldown buffs, helping Priests coordinate Power Infusion.

GETTING STARTED

1. Type /pia to open settings.
2. Under Requests, choose who can trigger tracked-cooldown alerts.
3. Under Activation, choose the Priest roles and content types where PI Alert
   is active.
4. Under Spells, enable the buffs you want to track.
5. Under Alerts, choose the glow, icon and aura-icon presentation.

TRACKED COOLDOWNS

PI Alert uses Blizzard's secure CustomAuraContainer system and configured helpful
aura spell IDs. A tracked spell must create a buff on its caster. Talent-dependent
entries show their requirements under the spell name.

REQUESTER RULES

You can allow everyone in your party or raid, only your focus, or a list of
specific players. Specific Players can fall back to your focus or everyone when
none of the listed players are present.

MACROS

The Macros page creates an account-wide General Macro named PI Alert. Player and
Focus variants fall back to a valid friendly mouseover target. Right-click a
party or raid member to manage Specific Players or set the Player macro target.

COMMANDS

/pia            Open or close settings
/pia mouseover  Create or update the Mouseover macro
/pia mo         Short Mouseover macro alias
/pia focus      Create or update the Focus macro
/pia test       Show a test alert
/pia reset      Reset settings
/pia help       Show the command list

SAVED SETTINGS

Settings are account-wide in PIAlertDB. Updating preserves supported settings;
obsolete request settings are cleaned up automatically.
